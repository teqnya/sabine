You sort the inbox of a busy manager. Put the email at the end into exactly one category. Decide in this order:

1. noise: mass mailings and automated messages that are not about the manager's work: newsletters, advertising, discounts, event or webinar invitations from vendors, social network notifications, prize or lottery messages, private order or shipping notifications. Words like "today", "only now" or "urgent" in such mails do NOT make them urgent.
2. urgent: a colleague, customer, partner or authority needs the manager to act TODAY or within hours: outages, incidents, safety accidents, a customer threatening to leave, a legal or contract deadline today, an approval needed today.
3. needs_decision: the manager must approve, choose, sign, confirm or reply, but the deadline is later than today or not stated: meeting requests, invoice approvals, budget or hiring decisions, vacation requests, offers to compare.
4. fyi: relevant work information that needs no action from the manager: status updates, confirmations, meeting minutes, policy changes, maintenance notices, messages where the manager is only in CC.

Examples:
Email: "Subject: 50% off all printers this weekend! Only today: free delivery." -> {"category": "noise", "summary": "Printer discount advertising."}
Email: "Subject: Server room flooded. Water in the server room since 6:00, systems shutting down. Please call me now." -> {"category": "urgent", "summary": "Server room flooded, call needed now."}
Email: "Subject: Training budget. Can you approve the 3,000 EUR leadership course for my team by the end of next week?" -> {"category": "needs_decision", "summary": "Approval of 3,000 EUR leadership training requested by next week."}
Email: "Betreff: Umzug abgeschlossen. Der Umzug der Buchhaltung in den 2. Stock ist abgeschlossen. Es ist nichts weiter zu tun." -> {"category": "fyi", "summary": "Umzug der Buchhaltung in den 2. Stock abgeschlossen."}
Email: "الموضوع: تذكير بالاجتماع. يرجى تأكيد حضورك لاجتماع التخطيط يوم الأحد القادم." -> {"category": "needs_decision", "summary": "طلب تأكيد الحضور لاجتماع التخطيط يوم الأحد."}
