You sort the inbox of a busy manager. Put the email at the end into exactly one category. Decide in this order and stop at the first category that fits:

1. urgent: a real person (colleague, customer, supplier, partner, lawyer, authority) describes a problem or deadline that needs the manager to act TODAY or within hours. Typical: outages and incidents, security incidents and data breaches, accidents, delivery stops, a customer threatening to cancel, a contract or legal deadline today, an approval needed today. A formal or calm tone does not make it less urgent.
2. noise: mass mailings and automated messages that are not about the manager's work: newsletters, advertising, discounts, vendor event or webinar invitations, social network notifications, prize or lottery messages, private order or shipping notifications. Words like "today" or "only now" in advertising do NOT make it urgent.
3. needs_decision: the manager must approve, choose, sign, confirm or reply, and the deadline is later than today or not stated: meeting requests, invoice approvals, budget or hiring decisions, vacation requests, offers to compare.
4. fyi: relevant work information that needs no action from the manager: status updates, confirmations, meeting minutes, policy changes, maintenance notices, messages where the manager is only in CC.

Examples:
Email: "Subject: Server room flooded. Water in the server room since 6:00, systems shutting down. Please call me now." -> {"category": "urgent", "summary": "Server room flooded, call needed now."}
Email: "Betreff: Brandalarm im Lager Nord. Die Sprinkleranlage hat ausgelöst, die Feuerwehr ist vor Ort. Wir brauchen bis 11 Uhr Ihre Entscheidung, ob die Spätschicht nach Hause geht." -> {"category": "urgent", "summary": "Brandalarm im Lager Nord, Entscheidung zur Spätschicht bis 11 Uhr nötig."}
Email: "Subject: 50% off all printers this weekend! Only today: free delivery." -> {"category": "noise", "summary": "Printer discount advertising."}
Email: "Subject: Training budget. Can you approve the 3,000 EUR leadership course for my team by the end of next week?" -> {"category": "needs_decision", "summary": "Approval of 3,000 EUR leadership training requested by next week."}
Email: "الموضوع: تذكير بالاجتماع. يرجى تأكيد حضورك لاجتماع التخطيط يوم الأحد القادم." -> {"category": "needs_decision", "summary": "طلب تأكيد الحضور لاجتماع التخطيط يوم الأحد."}
Email: "Betreff: Umzug abgeschlossen. Der Umzug der Buchhaltung in den 2. Stock ist abgeschlossen. Es ist nichts weiter zu tun." -> {"category": "fyi", "summary": "Umzug der Buchhaltung in den 2. Stock abgeschlossen."}
